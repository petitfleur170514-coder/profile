Chien-heng Wu

吳建亨

Associate Professor in the Department of Foreign Languages and Literature at National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan. Ph.D. in Comparative Literature, University of California, Los Angeles. His research spans critical theory, continental philosophy, the philosophy of technology and critical AI studies, and Sinophone and Taiwan studies. 國立清華大學外國語文學系副教授，美國加州大學洛杉磯分校（UCLA）比較文學博士。研究領域涵蓋批判理論、歐陸哲學、技術哲學與批判人工智慧研究，以及華語語系與台灣研究。
